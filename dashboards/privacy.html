Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 23, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction

Gravix ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our observability platform ("Service").

2. Data We Collect

Account Data

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored as a bcrypt hash). If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Stripe; we store only your Stripe customer ID.

Service Data

The Service ingests HTTP request metrics (service name, method, path template, status code, latency). We do not collect request bodies, user IDs, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable information from your monitored services.

Usage Data

We track event counts per tenant for billing purposes and may collect aggregated usage analytics to improve the Service.

4. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion request. Service data (request metrics) is retained according to your plan's retention policy. You may configure custom retention periods.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Settings → GDPR → Data Access)
  • Portability — download your data in machine-readable JSON format
  • Rectification — update your account information
  • Erasure — request account deletion with 30-day grace period
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Infrastructure providers — for hosting (data processing agreements in place)
  • Law enforcement — when legally required

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies only (session authentication). We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. You may manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords (bcrypt), API key hashing (SHA-256), rate limiting, and regular security reviews.

9. International Transfers

Data may be processed in jurisdictions outside your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for any international transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via email. Continued use after 30 days constitutes acceptance of updated terms.

11. Contact & DPO

For privacy inquiries or to exercise your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@gravix.io.
