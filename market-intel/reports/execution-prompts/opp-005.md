# CQC Audit-Ready Compliance Dashboard — Execution Brief

> Always inspection-ready: evidence management, staff training tracking, and audit-readiness scoring for independent UK care homes — at £149/month.

## Product Vision

### What it does
A web application for independent care home managers that maps uploaded evidence documents to the CQC Single Assessment Framework quality statements, tracks staff training with expiry alerts, maintains an incident log with CQC notification workflows, calculates an audit-readiness score, and simulates a CQC inspection checklist. Makes a small care home consistently "Always On" compliant rather than scrambling before inspections.

### Who it serves
Registered Managers and owners of independent residential and nursing care homes with 10-50 beds in England. The independent operator who can't afford Access Group, Radar Healthcare, or similar enterprise systems but faces the same CQC regulatory burden as operators who can.

### Why it matters
CQC's Single Assessment Framework (2023) replaced periodic inspection cycles with an "Always On" continuous readiness model. By 2026, the CQC operates continuously — data must be inspection-ready at any moment. 30% of social care workers cite excessive paperwork as a reason for leaving the sector. 85% of providers have some digital records but they are disconnected across systems. An independent 20-bed care home cannot justify £500+/month for Access Care Management but still faces the same regulatory framework. A 'Requires Improvement' or 'Inadequate' CQC rating can effectively close a care home — the stakes are existential.

## User Personas

### Primary: Registered Manager of an Independent Care Home
- **Context**: Manages a 15-25 bed residential home. Responsible for CQC compliance, staff management, care quality, and family relations simultaneously. Has a filing cabinet of evidence documents and a spreadsheet for staff training dates. Dreads unannounced CQC visits.
- **Pain points**:
  - Evidence for CQC's 5 key questions and 34 quality statements is scattered across systems
  - Staff training expiry dates tracked in a spreadsheet that's never fully current
  - Incident reporting to CQC is anxiety-inducing (what must be reported? by when? how?)
  - CQC inspection prep feels like an emergency every time — even though it shouldn't be
  - Last inspection report flagged "evidence not consistently evidenced across the service" — can't easily fix without a system
- **Success looks like**: Dashboard shows green/amber/red across all 34 quality statements. Can export an evidence pack for any quality statement in 5 minutes. CQC inspector arrives unannounced — no panic.

### Secondary: Care Home Owner (non-resident manager, owns 1-3 homes)
- **Context**: Has day-to-day management delegated but checks in on compliance weekly. Receives a Registered Manager's update but has no live view of audit-readiness.
- **Pain points**:
  - No real-time visibility into whether homes are compliant
  - Relies entirely on the RM's word that "everything is fine"
  - Would like a simple RAG dashboard across all their homes

## MVP Requirements (4-week scope)

### Core features
1. CQC framework library: all 34 quality statements (under 5 key questions: Safe, Effective, Caring, Responsive, Well-Led) with description and evidence guidance
2. Evidence upload and mapping: upload a document (policy, audit, survey, training record) → tag to one or more quality statements
3. Staff training tracker: employee roster + training records with type, date completed, expiry date, expiry alerts
4. Incident log: log incidents with CQC notification determination (which incidents must be notified to CQC, by when)
5. Audit-readiness score: calculate % of quality statements with at least one piece of evidence uploaded in the last 12 months
6. Pre-inspection checklist: 50-item checklist simulating typical CQC inspection focus areas
7. Evidence export: download all evidence for a specific quality statement as a ZIP

### User flows

**Flow 1: Upload and tag evidence**
1. Upload document (PDF, Word, image)
2. Select document type: policy, audit, survey, care plan review, training record, meeting minutes, etc.
3. Tag to quality statement(s): multi-select from the 34 CQC quality statements
4. System stores document and updates audit-readiness score
5. Evidence appears in that quality statement's evidence library

**Flow 2: Add staff training record**
1. Select employee (from roster)
2. Add training record: type (moving & handling, fire safety, safeguarding, medication, dementia awareness, etc.), date completed, expiry date (or mark as no expiry)
3. System calculates days until expiry, sets amber (60 days) and red (30 days) thresholds
4. Manager receives weekly email digest of expiring training

**Flow 3: Log an incident and determine CQC notification**
1. Log incident: date, time, resident involved (anonymised reference), description, immediate action taken
2. Incident type selector: fall, medication error, safeguarding concern, fire, unexplained injury, missing person, other
3. System determines: CQC notification required? (Yes: notify within 2 days / notify within 24 hours / No) — based on CQC Regulation 18 Notifications guidance
4. If required: reminder sent at deadline, notification status tracked (pending / submitted / overdue)
5. Incident saved to log with determination displayed

**Flow 4: Check audit-readiness**
1. Dashboard shows a radar chart or heatmap across the 5 key questions
2. Each quality statement shows: number of evidence documents, date of most recent upload, days since last evidence added
3. Red: no evidence or evidence older than 12 months
4. Amber: 1 piece of evidence, older than 6 months
5. Green: 2+ pieces of evidence, at least one in last 6 months

### Required screens/pages
- Dashboard: audit-readiness score (%), expiring training count, pending CQC notifications, recent evidence uploads
- CQC Framework: full list of 5 key questions and 34 quality statements with evidence count per statement
- Quality Statement detail: evidence list, upload button, guidance notes on what evidence is expected
- Staff training: employee roster, training record list, expiry calendar
- Incident log: chronological list, CQC notification status, export
- Pre-inspection checklist: 50 items, mark complete/N-A/incomplete
- Evidence export: select quality statement(s) → download ZIP

### Admin functionality
- Multi-home support (owner viewing across 1-3 homes)
- User roles: Owner (all homes), Registered Manager (one home), Deputy Manager (view only)
- Monthly readiness report email to owner/RM

### Authentication
- Email + password
- Single-home plan (one company) and multi-home plan (up to 5 homes)

### Billing
- £149/month per care home (single-home plan)
- £99/month per home for 2-5 homes (multi-home owner discount)
- 60-day free trial (longer than typical — care homes need time to populate evidence before the value is clear)
- Annual billing: 2 months free

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- Evidence upload with drag-and-drop (react-dropzone)
- Audit-readiness visualisation: recharts or simple CSS grid heatmap
- Vercel deployment

### Backend
- Next.js API routes
- PostgreSQL (Railway)
- Cloudflare R2 for evidence document storage
- Inngest for weekly training expiry digests and CQC notification deadline reminders

### Database
- `homes`: id, owner_id, name, cqc_registration_number, bed_count, billing_plan
- `quality_statements`: id, key_question (Safe/Effective/Caring/Responsive/Well-Led), statement_number, title, description, evidence_guidance (seeded from CQC framework)
- `evidence_documents`: id, home_id, doc_type, s3_key, upload_date, tags_json (quality statement IDs)
- `employees`: id, home_id, name, role, start_date
- `training_records`: id, employee_id, training_type, completed_date, expiry_date, provider
- `incidents`: id, home_id, date, type, description, cqc_notification_required, notification_due_date, notification_submitted_at
- `checklist_items`: id, home_id, item_text, category, status (complete/incomplete/na), last_updated

### API requirements
- **Claude API**: Evidence classification (which quality statements does this document relate to?). Optional enhancement — MVP can use manual tagging. Volume: 10-30 calls/day if AI tagging enabled. Cost: ~£5-15/month.
- **CQC public data** (no API, use static data): CQC publishes the 34 quality statements publicly. Seed these into the database on setup.
- **SendGrid/Resend**: Weekly training digests and incident notification reminders.

### AI model usage

**Evidence classification prompt (optional week 3+ feature):**
```
System: You are a CQC compliance expert. Given this care home document, identify which of the CQC Single Assessment Framework quality statements it most directly provides evidence for. Quality statements: [list of 34 statements]. Return JSON: {primary_statement_ids: number[], secondary_statement_ids: number[], confidence: "high"|"medium"|"low", reasoning: string}

User: Document title: [title]. Content excerpt: [first 500 words]
```

Use as a suggestion engine, not auto-tagger — manager always confirms the mapping. This is an enhancement to manual tagging, not a replacement.

**CQC Regulation 18 notification logic**: Entirely rules-based. Do NOT use AI for legal notification determination. Build a decision tree from the CQC guidance document.

- Vercel: £20/month
- Railway: £30-50/month
- Cloudflare R2: £5/month (evidence documents, typically PDFs)
- Total: ~£55-75/month

## Data Model

### Core entities
- **Home**: The care home. CQC registration number links to public CQC data.
- **QualityStatement**: Seeded from CQC framework. Immutable reference data.
- **EvidenceDocument**: Uploaded file tagged to quality statements. The core audit unit.
- **TrainingRecord**: Staff training completion and expiry. The most common compliance gap.
- **Incident**: Any notifiable or non-notifiable event. CQC notification determination stored.

### Important metadata
- Evidence documents retained 5+ years (CQC may ask for historical evidence)
- Quality statement mapping is append-only — removing evidence tags creates an audit trail gap
- Training records stored permanently — CQC inspectors check historical training completeness
- Incident descriptions anonymised for residents per GDPR (use resident reference IDs, not names)

## AI Usage Details

### Where AI adds value
1. **Evidence tagging suggestion**: Reduces the cognitive load of mapping documents to 34 quality statements — most managers don't know the framework well enough to tag confidently.
2. **Audit-readiness gap narrative**: "Your 'Safe' key question evidence is strong, but 'Responsive' only has 3 documents across 8 quality statements, most from 8 months ago" — a narrative summary is more useful than a score alone.

### Where deterministic systems are better
- **CQC notification determination**: Rules-based decision tree. Never AI. Legal deadlines are precise.
- **Audit-readiness score calculation**: Deterministic count + recency logic.
- **Training expiry calculation**: Calendar arithmetic.
- **Alert scheduling**: Rules-based, not AI.

## Go-To-Market

### First 10 customers

**The single most important early step**: Find 1-2 Registered Managers willing to co-develop. CQC compliance is trust-sensitive — you cannot sell this cold without social proof from an RMs peer network. A co-development partner gives you product validation, a case study, and a word-of-mouth entry point.

1. **CQC public inspection reports**: CQC publishes all inspection reports online including the Registered Manager's name. Search for homes with "Requires Improvement" ratings in your geography — they have the most acute pain. Email the RM directly.
2. **Care England**: Email their membership team. Offer a free 6-month trial for independent care home members.
3. **NCA (National Care Association)**: Focused on small independent operators — exactly your market.
4. **Facebook groups**: "Care Home Managers UK" (15,000+ members), "Registered Managers Forum". Post a question about CQC evidence management — don't sell immediately.
5. **LinkedIn**: Search "Registered Manager" + "care home" + UK. 5,000+ targets. Send personalised messages referencing the CQC "Always On" challenge.

### Outreach method
**Cold email to care home managers with recent "Requires Improvement" CQC rating:**
> Dear [Name], I noticed [Home Name] received a 'Requires Improvement' rating in [month]. One of the most common findings in CQC reports is inconsistent evidence management across the 34 quality statements. We've built a tool specifically for independent care homes — it maps your evidence to the CQC framework and shows you your audit-readiness score in real time. If you'd like a free 60-day trial, I'd love to hear what you think. [Signature]

**Facebook group engagement (not a sales post):**
> For any Registered Managers here — how do you currently keep track of your CQC evidence? Do you have a folder system, a spreadsheet, or something else? Trying to understand what the current state of the art looks like.

### Pricing
- £149/month per home: meaningful price that signals serious software (not a £9/month tool). CQC non-compliance risk is existential for a care home — they will pay for genuine compliance confidence.
- Multi-home discount (£99/home for 2-5 homes): targets the small group operator — a growing segment as independent homes consolidate.
- 60-day free trial: longer than typical because evidence population takes time. You want them to see value before billing starts.

### Validation before building
1. Read 20 CQC inspection reports from independent care homes with "Requires Improvement" ratings. Note which quality statements are most commonly cited as weak. This tells you which parts of the framework to prioritise in your evidence guidance.
2. Email 10 Registered Managers (find from CQC public reports) with a single question: "How long would it take you to gather evidence for a CQC inspection if one were announced today?" If more than half say "days" or "would be stressful", build it.
3. Attend one care home trade event or online webinar (Care Show, Skills for Care events are free/low cost). Speak to managers informally about their compliance process.
