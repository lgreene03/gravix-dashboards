## How to be listed

Open a pull request adding your row, or open an issue if you would rather we added it. Either way
the request has to come from someone at your organisation — we will not add an entry on someone
else's behalf, and the `Submission` column records the issue or pull request that authorised it.

We ask for: the organisation name, roughly when you started, your deployment shape, and approximate
scale. We do not ask for contract value, headcount, industry, or a sales contact. Being listed is a
technical fact, not a lead.

**You can ask us to remove your entry at any time, for any reason, and we will do it within 48 hours
without asking why.**
