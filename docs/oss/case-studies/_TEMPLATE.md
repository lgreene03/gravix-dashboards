# <Organisation> and Gravix

## Who

Who they are and what they run. Enough that a reader can tell whether their situation resembles
their own.

## The problem before Gravix

In their words, not ours. What was going wrong, how they noticed, and what it cost them.

## What they deployed

The actual configuration, with numbers. Stack (bootstrap or full), storage backend, how many
services, retention, roughly how many events.

## What worked

Specific outcomes, with figures where they have them. "Time to notice a regression went from hours
to minutes" is worth more than "improved observability", and a figure they are willing to share is
worth more than both.

## What did not work

**MANDATORY. At least one real item, and do not trim this section.**

Friction, a missing feature, a mistake we made, an hour lost to a confusing default, something they
expected and had to work around. If the subject says there was nothing, ask again — there always
is, and a study without this section is an advertisement that technical readers will discount
entirely, along with everything else on the page.

## What they would tell someone evaluating Gravix

Unedited. Their words, including the parts that are unflattering or that recommend they also run
something else.
