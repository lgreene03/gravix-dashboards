No data available

                
Gravix hasn't received any events yet. Send your first event:

                
curl -X POST http://localhost:8090/api/v1/facts \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-API-Key: $API_KEY" \
  -d '{
    "event_id": "'$(uuidgen | tr A-F a-f)'",
    "event_time": "'$(date -u +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)'",
    "service": "my-service",
    "method": "GET",
    "path_template": "/api/v1/health",
    "status_code": 200,
    "latency_ms": 42
  }'

                
Then wait for the rollup job to aggregate metrics (runs every 5 minutes).
