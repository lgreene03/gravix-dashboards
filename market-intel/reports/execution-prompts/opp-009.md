# UK SME Workplace Safety Compliance Tracker — Execution Brief

> Guided health and safety compliance for UK SMEs: AI-generated risk assessments, RIDDOR checker, PPE tracking, and inspection-ready documentation — all for under £70/month.

## Product Vision

### What it does
A web application that guides UK SME owners and managers through creating and maintaining required health and safety documentation: site-specific risk assessments, COSHH assessments, PPE records, incident logs with RIDDOR reportability checker, and an H&S policy library with annual review reminders. AI generates initial risk assessment drafts from a plain-English job description.

### Who it serves
Owner-managers and office administrators of UK SMEs in construction, manufacturing, warehousing, food production, and trades — with 5-50 employees. Currently relying on generic HSE Word templates, bought document packs, or nothing at all.

### Why it matters
45% of UK SME workplaces have H&S compliance gaps. Written risk assessments, COSHH assessments, and PPE records are a legal requirement under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974 and associated regulations. When HSE inspects — increasingly common post-pandemic — inability to produce current, site-specific documents results in improvement notices, prohibition notices, and fines of up to £20,000+. Enterprise H&S software (Evotix, Alcumus) costs £200-500+/month and is designed for large organisations. SMEs are underserved.

## MVP Requirements (4-week scope)

### Core features
1. Risk assessment builder: activity → hazard identification → control measures → responsible person → review date
2. AI risk assessment draft: enter job/activity description → AI generates initial hazard list and suggested controls → user reviews and edits
3. Incident log with RIDDOR reportability checker: enter incident details → system determines whether RIDDOR reporting is required and by when
4. PPE record tracker: employee → PPE items issued → date → next inspection/replacement date
5. H&S policy library: upload existing policies or select from templates → set annual review reminders
6. Compliance dashboard: red/amber/green for each area with overdue items highlighted

### User flows

**Flow 1: Create a risk assessment**
1. Choose: create from scratch, use AI draft, or pick from template library
2. If AI draft: enter activity description (e.g. "Working at height using scaffolding to repair roof tiles")
3. AI generates: list of hazards, severity/likelihood ratings, suggested control measures, PPE requirements, regulatory references
4. User reviews, edits, approves
5. Risk assessment saved with review date (default: 12 months or when activity/conditions change)
6. Alert scheduled for review date

**Flow 2: Log an incident**
1. Enter: date, time, location, persons involved, description of what happened
2. RIDDOR checker asks: Was anyone injured? What was the injury type? Was the person an employee or a member of the public? Days absent?
3. System determines: Not RIDDOR reportable / Reportable (specify: over-7-day, specified injury, dangerous occurrence, death)
4. If reportable: shows RIDDOR deadline, provides direct link to HSE's online RIDDOR form, records submission
5. Incident saved to log regardless of RIDDOR status

**Flow 3: Issue PPE to an employee**
1. Select employee
2. Choose PPE type (hard hat, hi-vis vest, safety boots, gloves, safety glasses, etc.)
3. Record: date issued, size/specification, replacement schedule
4. Employee PPE record updated
5. Alert when PPE replacement due

**Flow 4: H&S policy review reminder**
1. Upload existing policy PDF or choose from template
2. Set annual review date
3. System sends email reminder 30 days before review date
4. Manager opens policy, confirms reviewed, system logs date

### Required screens/pages
- Dashboard: compliance score, overdue items, upcoming reviews, recent incidents
- Risk assessments: list (activity, date created, last reviewed, status), create/edit flow
- Incident log: chronological list, RIDDOR determination badge, submission status

- Policy library: uploaded/templated policies with review dates
- RIDDOR guide: plain-English explainer of what must be reported and when

### Admin functionality

- Employee roster (for PPE assignment and accident reporting)
- Bulk document export (ZIP of all current H&S documents for HSE or insurance)

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- Mobile-friendly (incidents often logged from phones on-site)
- PWA for offline incident logging
- Vercel deployment

### Backend
- Next.js API routes
- PostgreSQL (Railway)
- S3 (Cloudflare R2) for policy document storage
- Inngest for review date reminders and PPE replacement alerts

### Database
- `companies`: id, name, industry, employee_count, billing_plan
- `risk_assessments`: id, company_id, activity, hazards_json, controls_json, review_date, last_reviewed_at, status
- `incidents`: id, company_id, date, description, injury_type, person_type, riddor_required, riddor_submitted_at
- `ppe_records`: id, company_id, employee_id, ppe_type, issued_date, replacement_due, notes
- `employees`: id, company_id, name, role, department
- `policies`: id, company_id, name, type, s3_key, review_date, last_reviewed_at
- `audit_log`: id, company_id, action_type, actor_id, details_json, created_at

### API requirements
- **Claude API**: Risk assessment draft generation. Estimated 20-50 drafts/day at launch. Use `claude-sonnet-4-6` for quality (risk assessments need accurate hazard identification, not just speed). Cost: ~£20-40/month.
- **HSE RIDDOR API**: No public API exists. RIDDOR reporting is done via HSE's web form. Build a pre-fill helper that populates an HSE URL with incident data (HSE supports this for some incident types).
- **SendGrid/Resend**: Review and replacement alert emails.

### AI model usage

**Risk assessment generation prompt:**
```
System: You are a UK health and safety expert. Generate a site-specific risk assessment for the following work activity. The business is a [industry] company in the UK with [employee_count] employees. Format as JSON with: {hazards: [{hazard: string, persons_at_risk: string[], severity: 1-5, likelihood: 1-5, risk_rating: "low|medium|high", existing_controls: string[], additional_controls: string[], ppe_required: string[], relevant_legislation: string}], review_period_months: number}

User: Activity: [user input]
```

**RIDDOR determination logic:** Entirely rules-based — do NOT use AI. RIDDOR has precise legal thresholds. Build a decision tree based on HSE guidance.

## Data Model

### Core entities
- **RiskAssessment**: Specific work activity with hazards and controls. Has review cycle.
- **Incident**: Any workplace accident, near miss, or dangerous occurrence. RIDDOR determination is stored as a flag.
- **PPERecord**: PPE item issued to an employee with replacement schedule.
- **Policy**: H&S policy document (uploaded or from template) with review date.

### Important metadata
- Risk assessments version-controlled — when edited, old version preserved (HSE may ask for history of changes)
- Incidents immutable once logged — corrections added as notes, not edits
- RIDDOR submission timestamp must be stored (HSE requires evidence of timely reporting)
- All documents exportable as ZIP for insurance or HSE inspection

## AI Usage Details

### Where AI adds value
1. **Risk assessment drafting**: The hardest part of H&S compliance for non-specialists is knowing what hazards to list. AI can generate a comprehensive hazard list from a plain description, saving hours of research.
2. **Control measure suggestions**: AI can suggest specific, relevant control measures and cite relevant UK H&S regulations.
3. **COSHH assessment (future feature)**: AI parses uploaded safety data sheets (SDS) and auto-fills COSHH assessment fields.

### Where deterministic systems are better
- **RIDDOR determination**: Rules-based decision tree, never AI. Wrong RIDDOR decisions carry legal consequences.
- **Review date calculations**: Calendar arithmetic.
- **Compliance score calculation**: Deterministic count of overdue items.
- **PPE replacement scheduling**: Rules-based calendar logic.

### Prompting suggestions
- Include industry context in every risk assessment prompt — a "working at height" RA for a window cleaner is different from one for a construction site.
- Ask AI to cite specific UK regulations (PUWER, LOLER, COSHH Regs, Working at Height Regs 2005) — this makes the output credibly professional.
- Always present AI output as a draft requiring human review — this manages liability and is accurate.
