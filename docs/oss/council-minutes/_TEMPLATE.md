**Comment window:** YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD (7 or 14 days per the tier)
**Council size at the vote:** N members

---

## The question

State it as it was actually asked, not as it looks in hindsight. If the question changed during the
discussion — and it often does — say so and give both forms.

## Positions

Every position that was argued, including the ones that did not prevail. This is the section a
future reader will care about, because the question will come back and they will want to know what
was already considered.

### For

> Summarise the argument, not the arguer.

### Against

> Same. A minute that records only the winning argument tells a reader what was decided and nothing
> about why it was close.

### Raised and not resolved

Anything that came up and was set aside. Say why it was set aside; "we ran out of time" is a valid
and useful answer.

## Vote

| For | Against | Abstained |
|---|---|---|
| N | N | N |

Threshold required: <from council.md §Decision thresholds>
Met: yes / no

Individual votes are **not** recorded unless a member asked to be. If any did, list only those
members, with a note that they requested it.

Abstentions caused by the one-third employer rule are noted here:

> `council: <org> holds <n>/<m> seats; <member> abstains on charter-tier votes`

## Outcome

What was decided, in one sentence. If it was a tie:

> `council: tied at <n>-<n>; the proposal does not proceed`

A tied vote fails and the proposal may be brought again with a better argument. It is not escalated
to anyone for a casting decision, because there is nobody who holds one.

## Follow-up

The issue, RFC or pull request this decision leads to. A decision with no follow-up is a decision
nobody has to act on, which is usually a sign the question was not ready.

## Charter check

Did this decision touch charter §7.1, §7.3 Q4 or §7.4?

- **No** — say so.
- **Yes, strengthening** — record the two-thirds threshold and the count.
- **Yes, weakening** — this vote should not have happened. Entrenched clauses cannot be weakened by
  any threshold; record the refusal instead:

  > `council: §<n> is entrenched and may be strengthened only`
