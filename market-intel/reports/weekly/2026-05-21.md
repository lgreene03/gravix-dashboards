# Market Intelligence Report — 2026-05-21

## Executive Summary

This is the first run of the market intelligence pipeline. 19 distinct web searches were conducted across UK/NI Reddit communities, government procurement (Contracts Finder, eTendersNI), regulatory change tracking (HMRC, CQC, FSA, DVSA, DEFRA), job postings, and industry news. **16 opportunities were discovered and scored.** The dominant theme this cycle is **regulatory-triggered compliance pain**: April 2026 CIS changes for construction, MTD ITSA for sole traders, CQC's "Always On" model for care homes, and PPDS updated guidance for food businesses are all creating acute, dateable pain points with identifiable customer populations. The top recommendation is **opp-002: NI Windsor Framework Trade Documentation AI** (3.91 overall) due to its unique geography, lack of direct competition, and very high willingness to pay — but it carries high build complexity. The lowest-friction starting point is **opp-007: Right-to-Work Automated Checker** (3.88) given its simple build, large market, and self-serve sales motion.

## Trends Detected

### 1. April 2026 as a regulatory forcing function
Multiple UK regulations took effect April 2026: CIS reforms, MTD ITSA for >£50k income, NHS dental contract changes, and tachograph extension to 2.5T vehicles. This creates a concentrated wave of compliance pain with identifiable trigger dates. Products that help businesses through a specific April 2026 requirement have built-in urgency.

### 2. Post-Brexit infrastructure gaps persist
CHIEF was decommissioned only in December 2024. The customs documentation space is still adapting. NI's dual EORI requirement and Windsor Framework complexity creates a specific, underserved niche with high willingness to pay (£48/declaration is a concrete cost to beat).

### 3. AI enabling document extraction at scale
Multiple opportunities (customs docs, allergen compliance, care home evidence, RTW documents) are made significantly more buildable by current AI document extraction capabilities. Claude API's ability to parse invoices, certificates, and mixed-format supplier declarations with high accuracy reduces MVP complexity substantially.

### 4. CQC "Always On" creating demand for continuous compliance tools
The CQC Single Assessment Framework shift from episodic inspection to continuous readiness is driving demand for care home compliance software. This is a multi-year tailwind; the market is not yet consolidated.

### 5. 80% RTW non-compliance is a remarkably high signal
The Home Office survey finding that 80% of employers answer RTW compliance questions incorrectly is unusually strong evidence of underserved need. Most compliance software markets are discovered through pain signals; an official government survey quantifying compliance failure rate is rare and actionable.

### 6. Construction software gap confirmed by BuildersAI own roadmap
BuildersAI explicitly stating "CIS planned for 2026" on their pricing page confirms that the leading UK-focused construction software for SMEs doesn't yet cover CIS. This is a direct market signal.

## Top 10 — Detailed Breakdowns

### 1. NI Windsor Framework Trade Documentation AI (opp-002) — Overall: 3.91

**Problem**: NI businesses need both GB and XI EORI numbers, determine goods-at-risk status per shipment, and comply with Windsor Framework rules unique to their geography. CHIEF decommissioned December 2024; average customs declaration costs £48. £1.8 billion total UK customs admin burden in 2022.

**Solution**: Web app that accepts commercial invoices and packing lists, uses Claude API to extract shipment data, runs a Windsor Framework eligibility decision tree, and drafts a CDS-compliant declaration ready for HMRC submission. Per-declaration pricing undercuts intermediaries (£8-15 vs £48).

**Why now**: Windsor Framework rules are still bedding in. CHIEF shutdown is recent. The HMRC TRE system launched March 2026 creates new reconciliation data. Market is in the adaptation phase before solutions consolidate.

**Key risk**: Maintenance burden is high — Windsor Framework rules actively evolving. Requires expert customs knowledge to build the decision tree correctly and keep it current.

**Next step**: Interview 5 NI exporters about their current customs process. Validate per-declaration pricing model vs. subscription preference.

### 2. Right-to-Work Automated Document Checker (opp-007) — Overall: 3.88

**Problem**: 80% of UK employers answer RTW compliance questions incorrectly (Home Office, April 2025). Civil penalties up to £60,000 per worker. Post-Brexit eVisa/share code system added complexity. High-turnover sectors track expiry in spreadsheets.

**Solution**: Web app: employee register, document upload with AI type recognition, UKVI share code integration, 90/60/30-day expiry alerts, audit trail. Built to pass a Home Office inspection.

**Why now**: Government enforcement is increasing. The UKVI share code system is still unfamiliar to many employers. The 80% non-compliance rate is exceptionally high evidence of market need.

**Key risk**: Specialist competitors (Uright, Xydus) exist. Must enter at lower price point and focus on self-serve simplicity vs. their compliance consultancy bundling.

**Next step**: Compare Uright and Xydus pricing/features directly. Identify the underserved size band (likely 10-50 employees who are too small for these tools).
