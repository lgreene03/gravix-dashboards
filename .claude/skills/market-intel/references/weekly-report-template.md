# Weekly Report Template

Save to `market-intel/reports/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## Structure

```markdown
# Market Intelligence Report — {Date}

## Executive Summary

{3-5 sentences: what changed this week, headline findings, key recommendation}

## New Opportunities Discovered

{For each new idea: one paragraph with problem, solution, initial score}

## Biggest Movers

### Rising
{Ideas that gained rank or score, with explanation of why}

### Declining
{Ideas that lost rank or score, with explanation of why}

## Trends Detected

{Cross-cutting patterns: regulatory shifts, technology enablers,
industry-wide movements, seasonal factors}

## Ranked Opportunity Table

| Rank | Delta | ID | Name | Industry | Geo | Opp | Build | Rev Vel | Overall | Competition | MVP Time | Price/mo | Confidence |
|------|-------|----|------|----------|-----|-----|-------|---------|---------|-------------|----------|----------|------------|
| 1 | +2 | opp-005 | ... | ... | UK | 4.2 | 3.8 | 4.0 | 4.01 | Low | 6 weeks | £99 | High |

## Top 10 — Detailed Breakdowns

### 1. {Idea Name} (opp-XXX) — Overall: X.XX

**Problem**: {One paragraph}
**Solution**: {One paragraph}
**Why now**: {One sentence}
**Key risk**: {One sentence}
**Next step**: {One sentence — what would validate this further}

{Repeat for top 10}

## Ideas Archived This Week

{Any ideas removed from active tracking, with reason}

## Research Gaps

{Areas where evidence is thin and more research is needed next week}
```
