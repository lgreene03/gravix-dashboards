# Construction Job Costing + CIS Invoicing — Execution Brief

> The missing mid-market tool for UK/NI builders: job costing, subcontractor management, and CIS-compliant invoicing in one place for under £150/month.

## Product Vision

### What it does
A mobile-first web app for small building contractors that tracks job budgets vs. actuals in real time, logs subcontractor payments with automatic CIS deductions, generates CIS-compliant invoices with correct reverse charge VAT handling, and shows per-job profit/loss — all without requiring the owner to be an accountant.

### Who it serves
Owner-managers of UK/NI construction firms with 5-30 employees, running multiple residential or light commercial jobs simultaneously. The person who currently cobbles together WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and Xero and still doesn't know if they made money on a job until their accountant tells them six months later.

### Why it matters
Enterprise construction software (Procore, Eque2) prices out at £500+/month and is designed for £20M+ turnover businesses. Generic accounting tools (Xero, QuickBooks) lack job costing depth. BuildersAI — the most credible UK-focused SME construction tool — explicitly does not have CIS features as of May 2026. The result: UK SME builders lose 5-10 hours/week reconciling disconnected systems and routinely discover they lost money on jobs only at year-end. April 2026 CIS liability changes make accurate CIS records an urgent compliance matter, not just an accounting nicety.

## User Personas

### Primary: Building Contractor Owner-Manager
- **Context**: Runs 3-8 concurrent jobs at any time. Has a mix of employed staff and subbies. Wife/partner or part-time office admin does the books in Xero. Has no live view of job profitability. Quotes jobs based on gut feel.
- **Pain points**:
  - Finds out a job lost money when accountant does year-end — too late to fix
  - CIS certificates are generated manually in a Word template
  - Can't quickly tell a customer what's been spent on their job
  - Doesn't know if a subcontractor is being paid the right CIS rate
  - Spends Sunday evenings chasing receipts via WhatsApp
- **Success looks like**: Opens the app on Monday morning, sees every job's current spend vs. budget, adds a payment to a subbie in 30 seconds, sends a CIS-compliant invoice in 2 minutes.

### Secondary: Office Admin / Bookkeeper
- **Context**: Handles invoicing and Xero data entry for the firm. Currently manually types subcontractor payment info into Xero and generates CIS certificates in Word. Sends these to the accountant monthly.
- **Pain points**:
  - Reconciling WhatsApp messages against actual payments is tedious
  - Reverse charge VAT invoices keep being formatted incorrectly
  - Accountant keeps asking for "the actual job costs" which don't exist systematically

## MVP Requirements (4-week scope)

### Core features
1. Job register (job name, customer, start date, budget breakdown by category)
2. Cost logging per job (labour, materials, subcontractor, plant — photo receipt upload optional)
3. Subcontractor payment with auto-CIS deduction (linked to subcontractor register with verified status)
4. CIS payment certificate generator (PDF, compliant format)
5. Invoice generator with CIS domestic reverse charge VAT handling
6. Job profit/loss dashboard (budget vs. actual, margin %)
7. Xero/QuickBooks export (CSV or native API)

### User flows

**Flow 1: Set up a job**
1. Create job: name, customer, address, contract value
2. Add budget categories: labour (£X), materials (£X), subcontractors (£X), plant (£X)
3. Job appears on dashboard with 0% spend

**Flow 2: Log a cost**
1. Select job
2. Choose category (labour / materials / subcontractor / plant)
3. Enter: amount, supplier/subcontractor, date, description
4. Optional: photograph receipt
5. Running total updates immediately; margin indicator turns amber if budget exceeded

**Flow 3: Pay a subcontractor**
1. Select subcontractor from register (pre-loaded with CIS verification status)
2. Enter gross payment, materials element if any
3. System calculates CIS deduction (0% / 20% / 30% per HMRC status)
4. Generate CIS payment certificate PDF
5. Payment recorded against job cost

**Flow 4: Raise an invoice**
1. Select job and customer
2. Enter invoice amount and description
3. Toggle: "Is this CIS domestic reverse charge?" — if yes, system adds correct VAT note
4. PDF invoice generated with all required fields
5. Invoice marked as sent; payment date tracked

**Flow 5: Check job profitability**
1. Dashboard shows all active jobs with traffic light (green / amber / red based on spend vs. budget)
2. Click job: see breakdown by category, subcontractor payments, invoices raised vs. costs incurred
3. Gross margin % shown per job

### Required screens/pages
- Dashboard: active jobs with status, total outstanding invoices, upcoming payments
- Job detail: budget vs. actual by category, cost log, payment certificates, invoices
- Add cost: quick-entry form, photo upload
- Subcontractor payments: list with CIS certificate links
- Invoice list: raised, sent, paid status
- New invoice: form with reverse charge toggle
- Subcontractor register: linked from opp-001 or standalone simplified version
- Xero export: one-click export for accountant

### Admin functionality
- Multiple user seats (owner + office admin)
- Customer address book (reuse across jobs)
- Subcontractor database (shared across jobs)

### Authentication
- Email + password
- Single company account with up to 3 seats on base plan

### Billing
- £49/month (up to 5 active jobs), £99/month (6-20 jobs), £199/month (unlimited)
- 30-day free trial
- Annual billing: 2 months free

## Technical Architecture

### Frontend
- Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- Mobile-responsive (contractors use phones on-site)
- PWA for offline cost logging (sync when back online)
- Vercel deployment

### Backend
- Next.js API routes
- PostgreSQL (Railway)
- S3-compatible (Cloudflare R2) for receipt photos
- PDFKit or Puppeteer for invoice and certificate generation

### Database
- `companies`: id, name, vat_registered, billing_plan
- `jobs`: id, company_id, name, customer_id, start_date, status, contract_value
- `budgets`: id, job_id, category (labour/materials/subcontractor/plant), amount
- `costs`: id, job_id, category, subcontractor_id (nullable), amount, date, description, receipt_s3_key
- `subcontractors`: id, company_id, name, utr, cis_status, deduction_rate
- `payments`: id, cost_id, subcontractor_id, gross_amount, materials_amount, deduction, certificate_pdf_key
- `invoices`: id, job_id, customer_id, amount, vat_type, sent_at, paid_at, pdf_key
- `customers`: id, company_id, name, address, email

### API requirements
- **HMRC CIS Verification API**: Same as opp-001 — apply for access on day 1.
- **Xero API / QuickBooks API**: OAuth integration for direct export. Start with CSV export in MVP to avoid API approval delays.
- **Claude API** (optional): Receipt photo → extract amount, supplier, date. Use `claude-haiku-4-5`. Volume: ~50 calls/day at launch. Cost: ~£3/month.

### AI model usage
- **Receipt OCR** (optional enhancement, not MVP): Photo of receipt → JSON extract {supplier, amount, date, category_guess}. Saves admin time on cost logging.
- **Job margin prediction** (future feature): Given job type, size, location, and team — predict likely margin based on historical jobs in the database.
- Core arithmetic (CIS deductions, VAT calculations) must be deterministic, never AI.

### Hosting
- Vercel: £20/month
- Railway: £30-50/month
- Cloudflare R2: £5/month (receipt photos)
- Total: ~£55-75/month

## Data Model

### Core entities
- **Job**: The central unit. Has budget, costs, payments, invoices.
- **Cost**: Any money going out on a job. May link to a subcontractor payment.
- **Payment**: A subcontractor payment with CIS calculation. Always links to a cost record.
- **Invoice**: Money coming in. Linked to job and customer.
- **Subcontractor**: Shared across jobs. Holds CIS status from HMRC verification.

### Important metadata
- CIS deduction rates must be stored at payment time (not just the current rate) — audit requirement
- Receipts stored 7 years minimum (HMRC record-keeping requirement)
- Soft deletes on jobs — contractors need historical data for tax purposes

## AI Usage Details

### Where AI adds value
- **Receipt photo extraction**: Converts a phone photo of a receipt into a pre-filled cost entry (supplier, amount, date). Biggest time-saver for on-site logging.
- **Category suggestion**: "This looks like a materials purchase at Travis Perkins — assign to Materials?" based on supplier name.

### Where deterministic systems are better
- CIS deduction calculation: strictly formulaic, always rules-based
- Reverse charge VAT logic: rules-based toggle with HMRC-defined text
- Invoice numbering: sequential, deterministic
- All financial arithmetic: never AI

### Fastest route to MVP
Skip HMRC live API in week 1 — allow manual CIS status entry (gross/standard/unmatched) per subcontractor. This lets you ship a working product in 3 weeks and swap in the live API when approved. The core job costing and invoicing value works without the API.
