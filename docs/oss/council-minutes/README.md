# Council minutes

Every decision the maintainer council takes, in public, within five working days.

**There are no minutes yet.** Gravix has one maintainer, so there is no council — see
[`../council.md`](../council.md). This directory exists so that the first decision has somewhere to
go on the day it is taken, rather than being minuted whenever somebody gets around to building the
place to put it.

## What a minute contains

Using [`_TEMPLATE.md`](_TEMPLATE.md):

- **The question**, stated as it was actually asked.
- **The positions argued**, including the ones that lost.
- **The vote count** — for, against, abstained.
- **The outcome**.
- **The follow-up** issue or RFC, so the decision leads somewhere checkable.

## What a minute does not contain

**Who voted which way**, unless a member asks to be recorded.

That is deliberate. Keeping the reasoning public is what makes the record useful to anybody reading
it later; keeping the individual votes private is what lets a member vote against their employer's
commercial interest without it becoming a permanent, searchable fact about them. A governance model
that makes an honest vote expensive will stop getting honest votes, and then the public reasoning
is public fiction.

A member who wants their vote recorded says so and it is recorded. The default protects the person
who needs it; the exception is available to the person who does not.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`, dated by the day the vote closed rather than the day it opened — a
decision is dated when it was made.

## Why five working days

Long enough to write something considered, short enough that the reasoning is still accurate rather
than reconstructed. A minute written a month later records what people remember deciding, which is
not the same thing.
