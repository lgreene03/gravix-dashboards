Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as an "event"?

One HTTP request fact sent to the ingestion API counts as one event. Each event includes method, path, status code, latency, and metadata. Batch requests are counted individually.

What happens if I exceed my event limit?

On the Free tier, events beyond the limit are rejected with a 429 status. On paid plans, events are accepted and billed at $1 per 100K overage events, so you never lose data.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing saves 20% compared to monthly. Toggle the switch above to see annual prices.

Is there a free trial?

Team and Business plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You'll be prompted to add payment before the trial ends.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in S3-compatible object storage in your selected region. Enterprise customers can specify custom storage locations. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Do you sign DPAs?

Yes. A standard Data Processing Agreement is available for all paid plans. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom terms. See our DPA template.
